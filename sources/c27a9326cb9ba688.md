## 2. VK Cloud / VK Tech — Специалист технической поддержки клиентов

Status: CONDITIONAL  
Fit: 97/100

Decision: do not mark PASS. Before prioritizing an application, get a direct answer to: are all 2/2 shifts daytime; exact shift hours; any overnight rotation; any after-hours/night on-call tied to the emergency process.
