# Russian IT vendor expansion — pass 01

Date: 2026-10-10

## Goal

Expand beyond the already well-covered Russian cloud/hosting market into infrastructure software, cybersecurity, networking, databases/data platforms and enterprise SaaS vendors that can employ remote Technical Support / Support Engineer / L1-L2 / operations-oriented engineers.

Hard filters remain unchanged:
- fully remote from Russia;
- no mandatory office/hybrid/onsite/travel;
- no night shifts or day/night rotation;
- no mandatory night on-call / regular after-hours;
- no phone-primary / call-center;
- no sales-heavy roles.
