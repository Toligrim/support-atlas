# Junior operations / infrastructure / monitoring research — batch 10

Date: 2026-10-10

## Goal

Broaden entry-level discovery beyond vacancy titles containing `technical support` into adjacent roles where the candidate's Linux/VPN/networking background can transfer directly:
- Junior DevOps;
- infrastructure / operations;
- monitoring / NOC;
- application support;
- incident/support engineering;
- junior sysadmin / SRE-adjacent roles.

Hard filters remain unchanged:
- fully remote and workable from Russia;
- no night shifts or day/night rotation;
- no mandatory after-hours/on-call;
- no phone-primary/call-center work;
- no mandatory onsite/travel;
- student/degree requirements are hard blockers where explicit.

## Scope and result

21 vacancy/role rows were recorded in `data/ru-junior-ops-infra-batch-2026-10-10-10.csv` after deduplication against earlier batches.

Result:
- PASS: 1
- CONDITIONAL: 5
- WATCH: 7
- REJECT: 8

The main finding is that broadening from support titles into junior infrastructure/DevOps produces several high-quality opportunities, while pure NOC/monitoring roles remain structurally poor for this search because 24/7 duty patterns are common.

## Market conclusion

Broadening titles was useful, but not because of NOC. The productive adjacent category is `Junior DevOps / infrastructure / application support`; pure monitoring and NOC frequently imply 24/7 duty schedules.

The candidate should therefore continue searching in two parallel lanes:
1. text-first technical/product support with Linux/API/networking;
2. junior infrastructure/DevOps roles that accept no commercial experience or practical personal-project experience.
