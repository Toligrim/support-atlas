# Junior / entry-level remote support research — batch 09

Date: 2026-10-10

## Goal

Shift vacancy-first discovery toward realistic entry roles: Junior / trainee / no experience / up to ~1 year, while keeping a technical bias toward Linux, DNS/networking, APIs, ServiceDesk, SaaS and troubleshooting.

Hard filters remained unchanged:
- fully remote and workable from Russia;
- no night shifts or day/night rotation;
- no mandatory after-hours/on-call;
- no phone-primary/call-center work;
- no sales-heavy duties;
- hard education/student-status requirements are blockers.

## Scope and result

19 vacancy/role rows were recorded in `data/ru-junior-entry-support-batch-2026-10-10-09.csv`.

Result:
- PASS: 3
- CONDITIONAL: 4
- WATCH: 3
- REJECT: 9

This batch is materially more useful for realistic entry probability than the previous middle/stretch-heavy passes. The main pattern remains that many nominally junior roles fail on phone-heavy work, night rotation, office requirements or student-only internship eligibility.
