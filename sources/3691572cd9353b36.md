# Russian SMB SaaS / API / integrations support — batch 05

Date: 2026-10-10

## Goal

Search small and mid-sized Russian SaaS, API, CRM/CDP/email, integrations and B2B-product companies for current remote support roles with a lower formal entry barrier and a higher chance of text-first support.

Hard filters stayed unchanged:
- fully remote and workable from Russia;
- no office/hybrid/onsite/travel requirement;
- no night shift / day-night rotation;
- no mandatory night on-call / after-hours duty;
- no phone-primary / call-center work;
- no sales-heavy duties.

## Scope and result

20 vendor/role rows were recorded in `data/ru-smb-saas-support-batch-2026-10-10.csv`.

Result:
- PASS: 1
- CONDITIONAL: 7
- REJECT: 6
- WATCH: 6

Published schedule metadata lists both 5/2 x 8 hours and 2/2 x 12 hours, but exact clock times are not public. Because nights are a hard exclusion, the role stays CONDITIONAL until a recruiter confirms that a fully daytime schedule can be selected and there is no night/on-call duty.

Evidence: https://dreamjob.ru/employers/86949/vakansii/136543922

## Decision

This batch validates the strategy of looking below the largest vendors. WAZZUP, Onpoint, Спикс and Chat2Desk are unusually well aligned with a text-first technical-support target, and WAZZUP has the lowest formal experience barrier among the strongest live leads.

The next search pass should continue in this direction: Russian SaaS/API/integration vendors, developer tools, self-hosted B2B products and niche vertical SaaS companies, while aggressively excluding generic voice customer support before doing deep verification.
