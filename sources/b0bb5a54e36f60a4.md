# Vacancy-first remote support research — batch 07

Date: 2026-10-10

## Goal

Switch from vendor-first discovery to broad vacancy-first search across the Russian web using role and stack markers such as `VPN`, `Linux`, `API`, `HelpDesk`, `Service Desk`, `tickets`, `chat`, `without calls`, `remote`, `L1`, `L2`, `Support Engineer`, then identify employers only after a relevant role is found.

Hard filters remained unchanged:
- fully remote and workable from Russia;
- no office/hybrid/onsite/travel requirement;
- no night shifts or day/night rotation;
- no mandatory after-hours/on-call duty;
- no phone-primary/call-center work;
- no sales-heavy role.

## Scope and result

30 vacancy/role rows were recorded in `data/ru-vacancy-first-batch-2026-10-10-07.csv`.

Result:
- PASS: 1
- CONDITIONAL: 5
- REJECT: 18
- WATCH: 6

The high REJECT share is expected and useful: vacancy-first search surfaces many superficially relevant Linux/API/support roles that can be eliminated immediately once schedule and channel evidence is checked.

- troubleshooting and diagnosis;

Required technical profile is unusually close to the candidate:
- Linux;

- Docker;

PostgreSQL is a plus rather than the core requirement.

Evidence:
- https://finder.work/vacancies/36826702
- https://dreamjob.ru/employers/6287440/vakansii/137967458

Decision: apply now. Zoom exists, but there is no evidence that this is phone-primary support or a call-center workflow.

## Strategy conclusion

Vacancy-first is worth continuing. It found EvaTeam and Цифра, which had not surfaced through the earlier vendor-first passes, while also exposing many false-positive technical-support jobs that fail only after checking night rotation/on-call/phone requirements.

For the next pass, keep the same strategy but expand queries around:
- `Linux support remote 5/2`;
- `API support remote`;
- `application support junior remote`;
- `Service Desk Linux remote`;
- `technical support tickets without calls`;
- `support engineer SaaS remote`;
- `product support junior remote`;
- `VPN support remote`;
- `hosting support tickets remote`.

Priority should remain current vacancies from the last 7–30 days, followed by employer-original verification for any role promoted to the active application shortlist.
